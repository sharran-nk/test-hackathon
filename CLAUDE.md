AI is a bonus, never a gate: the app must work without it.- Small and finished beats ambitious.- Hand-ins: SOLUTION_DESIGN.md (1-2 pages), hosted working app, README.md, Evaluator submission, short demo.- Scored on: Solution Design 20, Implementation Quality 20, Requirements Coverage 15, Security & Privacy 15, Testing & Verification 15, Operability & Reliability 10, Communication Clarity 5. Each rated 1-6. Only what is in the files earns credit.- The panel asks: 10x volume? third-party APIs banned? why build, not buy?## Time boxDesign 1.5h, build and host 2.5h, Evaluator submission 30m, demo prep 30m.## About me
